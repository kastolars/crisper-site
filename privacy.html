Crisper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24

Crisper holds a short list of fresh food that needs using soon. It is not a pantry inventory.

Everything this app stores stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud.

It makes no network connections at all. It contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go.

Deleting the app deletes its data. There is no copy to restore from.

This app reports the dates you entered and how many days have passed. What to do with the food is your decision.

Camera

Crisper includes an optional camera-based date scanner. It reads a date printed on a package using on-device text recognition; no image is ever stored, transmitted, or leaves the device. Camera access is requested only when you tap the scan control, and typing a date works identically whether or not you grant it.

Contact

Crisper is built by Karol Stolarski. Questions about this policy, or about the app, can be sent to crisper.support@proton.me.
