Crisper

A short list of food that needs using soon.

Crisper holds about fifteen things: the fresh food in your house with a clock on it. It is not a pantry inventory, an account isn't required, and it has no cloud of any kind. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Common questions

Does Crisper need an account?
No. There is no sign-in anywhere in the app.
Does it collect any data?
No. Crisper makes no network connections at all, contains no analytics and no third-party code. See the privacy policy for the full statement.
What happens if I delete the app?
Its data goes with it. There is no copy to restore from.
How does the reminder work?
At most one quiet notification a day, listing what needs using soon. Nothing louder, and often none at all.
Where do the typical windows come from?
The typical windows Crisper suggests are general guidance, drawn from published food-storage sources such as the USDA FoodKeeper and rounded for simplicity. They are estimates that depend on how your food was stored and handled, and they are not a guarantee about any food. The package, and your own judgement, come first. Crisper is not affiliated with or endorsed by the USDA.
What does the camera scanner do?
It reads a printed date on a package using on-device text recognition. Nothing is photographed, saved, or sent anywhere, and typing a date always works as an alternative.
